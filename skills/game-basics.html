Game IQ

Game Basics

Next Cue word — say only this while they move.

Why it matters

8U kids need a tiny playbook: where to stand, where to throw, what a force out is, and how to stay safe. Clear > clever.

How to

  1. Know your starting spot (position cone) and face the batter in Popcorn ready.
  2. Field it → throw to first unless a coach yells a different base.
  3. Force out: when the runner must go, tag the base with the ball (feet on bag).
  4. Safety always: helmet on in the box and on bases; bats only in the circle; stay in the dugout unless on deck.

Common mistakes

Everyone chasing the ball (no one covers a base)
Freeze a play mid-demo: "Who has first?" Assign covers before the next rep.
Throwing to the wrong place in a panic
Default rule for new players: "When in doubt, to first." Celebrate that choice.
Swinging bats in the dugout / no helmet on bases
Team rule, enforced kindly every time. One reminder = sit one pitch.

Coach station drill

Practice · 5 min Where Does It Go?

Setup: Full infield spots with cones; coach with a ball (no bat needed).

How: Call a scenario ("Grounder to short — force at first!"). Kids field a roll and throw to the right base. Reset fast. 6–8 scenarios. Keep energy high; correct with one cue word.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Kitchen Diamond

Gear: 4 placemats/shoes as bases, a soft ball

How: Walk through force outs vs. tag plays with one "runner" (parent) and one fielder (child). Ask: "Where do you throw?" before each roll. End with helmet/bat safety quiz (3 questions).

Level up

🚀 Child coaches the parent through one force-out play using the cue words alone.
