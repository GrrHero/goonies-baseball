Encourage without pressure

  • Ask before you tell: “What made that throw feel good?”
  • One cue only. Three tips = none stick.
  • End on a win. Last rep should be succeed-able.
  • Compare to yesterday, not to teammates.
  • Your face matters. Take a water break if you need a reset.
  • Fun is the retention strategy. Fighting? Shrink the task.

Safety at home

  • Foam / wiffle / tennis near windows, cars, and siblings.
  • No hard baseballs for soft toss without a net and space.
  • Helmet for anything game-speed with a real bat and harder ball.
