Crow Hop

Why it matters

After fielding, a small crow hop loads the body so the throw to first has zip without arm-only effort. Keep it a scoot — not a long jump.

How to

  1. Field the ball and bring it to the belly/chest.
  2. Small hop forward; back foot lands sideways (skateboard stance).
  3. Front foot steps straight at the target (usually first base).
  4. Throw and finish to the opposite pocket; stay balanced.

Common mistakes

Huge hop that throws them off balance
"Mouse hop, not kangaroo." Mark two shoe-widths and hop only that far.
Hopping before they have the ball
"No tock without a tick." Throw only counts after a clean field.
Hopping toward home instead of first
Lay a rope/line from fielding spot toward first: hop along the line.

Coach station drill

Practice · 5 min Scoot to the Bucket

Setup: Roller coach + pile of balls; laundry basket/bucket at "first" ~30–40 ft. Groups of 3–4 rotate in.

How: Roll a grounder. Kid fields, scoots, throws to the bucket. Next kid is already in ready. 4–5 reps each then rotate.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Tick-Tock to the Chair

Gear: Glove, soft balls, laundry basket on a chair as first base

How: Parent rolls grounders. Child says "tick" on the catch and "tock" on the crow-hop throw into the basket. First to 7 outs wins.

Level up

🚀 Field a ball one step to either side, then crow-hop throw without resetting the feet first.
