Why it matters

Accurate throws get outs and keep teammates safe. At 8U, kids need a simple pattern: aim, step, throw over the top — not a push or sidearm dart.

How to

  1. Grip: fingertips on the seams (two or four seam is fine); not deep in the palm.
  2. Stand sideways; glove-side shoulder and glove point at the target.
  3. Show the ball back (away from the head), step with the front foot toward the target.
  4. Throw over the top; finish with the throwing hand by the opposite pocket.

Common mistakes

Facing the target square and pushing the ball
Put feet on a line aimed at the target. "Look over your glove shoulder."
Stepping with the wrong foot (same as throwing arm)
Place a sock/cone in front of the glove-side foot: "Step on the sock."
Sidearm or stopping the arm at release
"Throw over a fence" and touch the opposite pocket after every throw.

Coach station drill

Practice · 5 min Partner Target Lines

Setup: Two parallel lines of kids, 20–30 ft apart (closer for newer throwers). One ball per pair.

How: On "Point!" kids freeze aimed; on "Throw!" they complete the throw. Rotate partners every 6 throws. Coaches walk the middle cueing — not long lines waiting.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Knock Down the Castle

Gear: Tennis/foam balls, empty cups or water bottles, a fence/wall target

How: Stack a cup castle on a bench. Child uses Point-Step-Throw from 15–25 feet. Score: knock-downs. Step back after 3 hits in a row.

Level up

🚀 Add a small crow-hop ("Scoot") before the throw, and finish balanced for 2 seconds.
