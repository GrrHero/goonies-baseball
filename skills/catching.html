Why it matters

Catching builds confidence. Start close and soft so kids succeed often; thumbs together for high balls, pinkies together for low balls, two hands always.

How to

  1. Eyes track the ball all the way into the glove.
  2. High ball: thumbs together, fingers up (like a window).
  3. Low ball: pinkies together, glove down (bowl / alligator bottom).
  4. Two hands: throwing hand covers / secures as soon as it hits.

Common mistakes

Turning the head or closing eyes
Use a foam/tennis ball and start 6–8 feet away. Success first, distance later.
One-handing every catch
Only award a point when the bare hand covers. Scoring coaches better than lectures.
Glove flipping the wrong way (fingers down on a high toss)
Freeze before the toss: "Show me window or bowl?" Then toss.

Coach station drill

Practice · 5 min Catch Ladder Partners

Setup: Pairs with tennis balls across the infield grass.

How: Start close. Every clean catch = one step back; a drop = one step forward. 90 seconds, then new partner. Coaches float and spot-fix glove shape.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Couch Ladder

Gear: Soft ball or rolled socks, glove optional

How: Underhand tosses from a few steps away. Eyes on it + two hands. Step back on catches. Mix high (window) and low (bowl) on purpose.

Level up

🚀 Catch 5 in a row, then take three giant steps back and try again.
