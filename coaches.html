Sample 60-minute practice

12 kids · 3–4 coaches · groups of 3–4 so nobody waits

TimeBlockNotes
0–5Arrive + warm-upJog, skips, Popcorn freezes. Helmets stay dugout-side until hitting.
5–8Team huddleOne theme cue for the day. Freeze signal review.
8–20Station A — Throw / Crow hopPartner lines + Scoot-to-bucket.
20–32Station B — Grounders / CatchAlligator Wave + Catch Ladder. Split groups.
32–44Station C — Hitting carouselTee + soft toss. On-deck = dry Squish freezes only.
44–54Game-likeWhere Does It Go? or Break the Tape.
54–60Cool-downWater + specific shout-outs.

Freeze signal

Pick one signal for the season (both hands up, or a long whistle). Feet stop, eyes on coach, mouths quiet. Practice it once every practice like a drill.

Praise that works

  • Praise the cue: “Great Chomp — glove ate grass!”
  • Praise effort louder than highlight-reel plays.
  • Be specific and short. Public praise, private fix.
  • Correct with the cue word while they move — not a lecture after.

Mixed levels & short attention

  • Stations beat lines. More than 3 waiting? Split.
  • Same drill, different distance. Newer closer; advanced step back or add Scoot.
  • One cue per activity.
  • 90-second rule. Change the constraint before boredom hits.

Safety checklist

  • Helmets on in the box and on the bases.
  • Bats only in the on-deck / hitting circle.
  • Soft toss / front toss: foam or wiffle; adult off the swing line.
  • No kids behind throw targets or under fly-ball paths.
  • Water every station; know the med kit + contacts.
