Hitting

Hitting

Squish the Bug Cue word — say only this while they move.

Why it matters

Coach-pitch success starts with a balanced stance, a simple load, and eyes on the ball. Tee and soft toss build the swing before live arm speed.

How to

  1. Stance: feet wider than shoulders, athletic bend, eyes level.
  2. Load / rock: hands and weight shift slightly back (quiet, not a huge step).
  3. Stride soft toward the pitcher; swing level through the ball.
  4. Back foot rotates — "squish the bug" — and finish balanced with belly button toward the field.

Common mistakes

Stepping in the bucket (away from the plate) or falling over
Tee work with a line under the feet. "Squish and freeze" for 2 seconds after contact.
Uppercutting / eyes pulling off early
Tee at mid-thigh; cue "Eyes on the ball" through contact. Soft toss from the side.
Giant load that ruins timing
Shrink the load to a tiny rock. Rhythm: "Rock… step… squish."

Coach station drill

Practice · 8–10 min (station) Tee + Soft-Toss Carousel

Setup: 2 tees + 1 soft-toss net/open space; groups of 3–4 per station.

How: 5 swings each, then rotate stations. Waiting kids shag or on-deck freeze swings (no live bats in the crowd). One coach per station; short lines only.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Driveway Tee / Soft Toss

Gear: Tee or stacked bucket, wiffle/foam balls, bat, open space away from windows

How: 10 tee swings with Squish the Bug. Then 8 soft tosses from a parent kneeling slightly off to the side (foam only). Freeze the finish each time.

Level up

🚀 Call a target (up the middle / opposite field) before the toss and try to hit that gate.
