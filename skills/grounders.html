Fielding

Fielding Grounders

Chomp Cue word — say only this while they move.

Why it matters

Most 8U balls are on the ground. Glove in the dirt out in front, top hand chomps like an alligator, then up to the belly — that sequence prevents "through the gate" misses.

How to

  1. Start in Popcorn ready; see the ball off the bat / roll.
  2. Move so the ball comes to the middle of your body.
  3. Glove down to the grass out in front (fingers touch dirt).
  4. Top hand CHOMPS on top; gather to the belt / belly.

Common mistakes

Glove up so the ball rolls under ("through the gate")
"Glove eats grass first." Place a leaf in the glove they must show you before the roll.
Fielding between the feet / under the body
Put a cone a half-step in front of the toes: "Chomp it at the cone."
Reaching with the glove while feet stay planted
Roll slightly to the side and celebrate when feet shuffle first: "Middle!"

Coach station drill

Practice · 5 min Alligator Wave

Setup: Two coaches rolling; kids in two short lines of 3–4 (or a semicircle if one coach).

How: Roll straight, slow grounders. Kid chomps, flips back, goes to the end. Next kid is already ready. 8–10 reps each. No 10-kid single line — split groups.

10-minute at-home drill

Home · 10 min Fish in the Swamp

Gear: Glove, tennis balls, open driveway/yard

How: Parent rolls "fish." Child is the alligator. Count fish eaten. Balls that get past swim to the ocean (parent scores). First to 10.

Level up

🚀 After a clean chomp, crow-hop throw to a bucket "first base" for a bonus point.
