Open the matching skill card for gear lists and level-ups. Skip a day if energy is gone — consistency over perfection.

Encourage without pressure

  • Ask before you tell: “What made that throw feel good?” lets them own the learning.
  • One cue only. If you give three tips, they hear none.
  • End on a win. Last rep should be something they can succeed at.
  • Compare to yesterday, not to teammates. “Farther than Monday” beats “why can’t you…”
  • Your face matters. Kids read frustration faster than words. Take a water break if you need a reset.
  • Fun is the retention strategy. If it’s a fight, shrink the task or switch to catch.

Safety at home

  • Foam / wiffle / tennis balls near windows, cars, and siblings.
  • No hard baseballs for soft toss unless you have a net and space.
  • Helmet if you’re doing anything game-speed with a real bat and harder ball.
